# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Harassment, insults,
sexualized attention, threats, and publishing others' private information are
unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact options.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
